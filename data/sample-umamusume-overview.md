## What is Umamusume: Pretty Derby?

Umamusume: Pretty Derby is a training-and-racing simulation game developed by Cygames.
In the Global version you raise "Uma Musume" (horse girls) through a three-year training
program, entering them in races and building their stats and skills along the way.

## Training stats

Each trainee has five core stats:

- **Speed** – how fast she can run; the most important stat for most races.
- **Stamina** – how long she can keep her top speed; crucial for long-distance races.
- **Power** – acceleration, and performance on slopes and in heavy ground.
- **Guts** – resistance to losing speed late in a race; helps in close finishes.
- **Wit (Intelligence)** – skill activation chance, race positioning, and recovery.

You increase stats by assigning training sessions. Each session can also trigger events,
restore energy, or grant skill hints depending on the support cards in your deck.

## Support cards

Support cards are equipped in a deck of six before a training run. They come in several types:

- **Speed, Stamina, Power, Guts, and Wit** cards boost the matching training.
- **Friend** cards give unique bonuses and a guaranteed bond.
- **Group** cards offer flexible effects.

Support cards have their own level, limit break level, and unique effects. A higher bond
(synergy) with your trainee increases the bonuses they provide.

## Races and the URA Finals

During training, your trainee enters races to earn fans. The story campaign ends with the
**URA Finals**, a series of three races (qualifier, semifinal, and final) held at the end of
the three-year program. Winning the URA Finals requires solid stats, a good skill set, and a
suitable race strategy.

Race strategies include Runner, Leader (Front Runner), Betweener (Pace Chaser), Chaser, and
End Closer. Matching a trainee's aptitude to a strategy and distance is key.

## Skills

Skills are purchased with skill points earned from training and events. They fall into
categories such as acceleration, recovery, speed boost, debuff, and lane-change skills.
Most skills have activation conditions (for example, "only in the final corner"), so picking
skills that match your race plan matters more than raw quantity.

## Glossary

- **Trainee** – the Uma Musume you are raising in a training run.
- **Deck** – the set of six support cards used during a run.
- **Bond / Synergy** – the relationship level between your trainee and a support card.
- **Aptitude** – a trainee's grade (S, A, B, ...) for a race distance, surface, or strategy.
- **Career / Story** – a single training run from debut to the URA Finals.
